# Project Features Guide

The Live Stock Market Simulator is designed to provide a realistic trading experience with advanced analytical tools.

## 1. Real-Time Trading Engine

- **Socket.io Integration**: Live price updates that move with the market.
- **Weighted Average Costing**: Automatically calculates buy price average when purchasing multiple lots of the same stock.
- **Instant Execution**: Seamless buy/sell flow with wallet balance verification.

## 2. Advanced AI Intelligence

- **Portfolio Health Score**: AI-driven score (1-100) based on your investment strategy.
- **Risk Assessment**: Identifies sector exposure and potential concentration risks.
- **Automated Sentiment**: Scans headlines to provide a Bullish/Bearish pulse of the market.
- **Trend Forecasts**: Interactive visualizations of predicted price movements.

## 3. Indian Market Indices

- Real-time tracking of:
  - **NIFTY 50**
  - **SENSEX**
  - **BANK NIFTY**
  - **NIFTY IT**
- Automatic detection of IST market hours.

## 4. User Experience

- **Glassmorphism UI**: Modern, sleek interface with blur effects and vibrant gradients.
- **Mobile Responsive**: Fully adaptive design for trading on the go.
- **Smart Notifications**: Real-time toast notifications for trade confirmations and alerts.
- **Leaderboard**: Global competition to see who can build the highest net value.
